# Philosopher Stones

Relic progression for **Pokémon Red Earth: The Philosopher's Stones**.

## Lesser Stones

These awaken from badge progress and teach the binding system:

- **Ruby Stone** — Boulder Badge — +15% outgoing damage.
- **Sapphire Stone** — Cascade Badge — -15% incoming damage.
- **Emerald Stone** — Rainbow Badge — restores 1/16 max HP after each completed battle turn.
- **Amethyst Stone** — Marsh Badge — 10% chance to rescue a move that would otherwise miss.

## Greater Stones

These are discoveries, not automatic badge rewards:

- **Moonstone** — return to **Mt. Moon B2F** after the Cascade Badge with a normal Moon Stone. The ordinary Moon Stone is consumed as the catalyst. Effect: cleanses the first major status condition suffered each battle.
- **Obsidian Stone** — return through **Pokémon Tower 7F** after the restless Marowak spirit has departed. Effect: once per battle, survive an otherwise lethal hit at 1 HP.
- **Solar Stone** — return to **Pokémon Mansion B1F** after earning the Volcano Badge. Effect: while active, Fire damage is +25% and Water damage is -25%.
- **Tempest Stone** — investigate the **Power Plant** after defeating/capturing Zapdos. Effect: Electric damage +20%; paralysis is immediately cleansed.

Each Greater Stone reveals an alchemical essence in the **ALCHEMY** start-menu journal:

- Obsidian → **Salt**
- Solar → **Sulfur**
- Tempest → **Mercury**
- Moonstone → **Aether**

Those essences are progression knowledge; the Greater Stones are not consumed.

## Binding

Open a Pokémon's party submenu and choose **STONE**. One physical relic can resonate with only one Pokémon at a time. The binding is stored on the Pokémon itself, so it follows that Pokémon through party reordering, PC storage, evolution and save/reload.

The stones do not consume move slots or replace species data, keeping them compatible with large Pokédex/moveset overhauls.
