# Philosopher Stones

A small relic system for **Pokémon Red Earth: The Philosopher's Stones**.

Four key-item stones awaken as Kanto badges are earned:

- **Ruby Stone** — Boulder Badge — +15% outgoing damage.
- **Sapphire Stone** — Cascade Badge — -15% incoming damage.
- **Emerald Stone** — Rainbow Badge — restores 1/16 max HP after each completed battle turn.
- **Amethyst Stone** — Marsh Badge — 10% chance to rescue a move that would otherwise miss.

Open a Pokémon's party submenu and choose **STONE** to bind or remove a relic. A physical stone can resonate with only one Pokémon at a time. The binding is stored on the Pokémon itself, so it follows that Pokémon through party reordering, PC storage, evolution and save/reload.

The stones do not consume a move slot and do not alter species data, which keeps them compatible with large Pokédex and moveset overhauls.
