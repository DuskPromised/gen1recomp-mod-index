# Take the Last Starter

A small Gen1Recomp mod for Pokémon Red/Blue.

After you choose a starter in Oak's Lab and your rival takes his counter-pick,
the third Poké Ball remains on the table. This mod changes that ball from
Oak's normal "last Pokémon" line into a one-time offer to take it as your
second starter.

## Shiny compatibility

The second starter is awarded through Gen1Recomp's normal `give_pokemon`
command. **Shiny Gifts & Starters** therefore sees it exactly like the first
starter. If **SHINY STARTERS** is enabled, the second starter receives the
same real shiny-DV treatment.

This mod does not change wild shiny odds.

## Safety

- The gift can only be claimed once.
- The ball is hidden only after the Pokémon is successfully stored.
- If both party and PC storage are full, the ball remains available.
- Before the first starter is chosen, the native Oak's Lab flow is untouched.
- The rival's starter choice and battle party are untouched.
